About the gym

A home for everybody.

A first class gym in the heart of the Tankerton community since 2001.

Our story

“Our members tell us it’s like one big fitness family.”

Well, what can we say? We love the gym! We have been working in the fitness industry for 30 years and running Flex Appeal in Tankerton since 2001. Fitness is a way of life for us, and we have seen the industry grow and change.

Our mission has been to provide our community with a first class gym, highly experienced and friendly staff, great customer service and above all a home for everybody that walks through the door. Whether you are looking to lose a few pounds, train for a specific goal or simply get together with friends and help each other on, we have something for everyone.

Many gyms can be intimidating or ‘showy’, but we focus on you, and new members soon find themselves happily part of the Flex Appeal team. Gyms come and go, but we believe putting fitness and our business in the heart of the community is the most important thing. After all, we have been doing that since 2001.

Supplements

We do supplements.
